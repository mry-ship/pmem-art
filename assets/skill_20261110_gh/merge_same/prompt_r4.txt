A 4:3 product catalog sheet on a solid dark gray background. Exactly eight equal white rounded-rectangle cards in a strict 2 rows by 4 columns grid that fills the frame evenly. Same card size, even gutters, no title, no text, no numbers, no logo, no UI, no finger, no food, no lobster. One pale celadon watering can per card, centered, inside the card. Opaque image. Glossy cartoon 3D, chunky, no hard outlines, warm saturated, soft light. Do not copy the reference board.

Every can has a tall empty arched bail handle. Not a round teapot. Not a solid gold can. No face.

SIZE is mandatory: heights about 34, 44, 54, 64, 74, 80, 86, 92 percent of each card. The bottom-left can is taller than the top-right can. Never shrink a later can.

SPOUT RULE: cards 1, 2, 3, 4, 5, and 6 end in a smooth open oval mouth like a hose. Zero holes, zero dots, zero sieve. Cards 7 and 8 are the only ones allowed a round sprinkler with holes.

TWO SPOUTS: cards 5, 6, 7, and 8 each have one spout pointing left and one spout pointing right, both long and separate. Cards 1 to 4 have only the right spout.

1 smallest plain open spout and handle only.
2 taller, copper ring around the open spout.
3 taller, keep ring, add a big green leaf. Open mouth, no holes.
4 taller, keep ring and leaf, add a large cream bow. Open mouth.
5 taller than card 4, keep bow leaf ring, ADD a left spout so two open tubes show.
6 taller, keep two open spouts, bow, leaf, ring, and add a copper pump in front of the empty handle.
7 taller, right spout gains a perforated sprinkler rose, left spout stays open. Handle, bow, leaf, ring, pump remain.
8 largest celadon green body with gold engraved flowers on the green, not solid gold. Two spouts, rose on the right one, handle, bow, leaf, ring, pump all visible.
