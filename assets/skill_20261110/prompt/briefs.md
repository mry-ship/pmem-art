## 方案 A · 窗边中景 · `route_id=draw.ordinary`

- **中文目标句**：画一张普通插画，妖精花园的木窗内侧，一只小妖精坐在窗台上写信，取中景，人物约占画面六成，头顶留出窗框的安全边，窗外花园只虚成远层。午后的暖侧光从左扇窗打进来，窗台上落下短而暖的接触影。媒介用不透明水粉，表面哑光，边缘比湿水彩更实，结构仍然连得住。完成度对标绘本立绘，湿画透气，不要霓虹，不要油腻塑胶高光。发色、瞳色和翅膀都不发明；信纸上只有浅淡、读不出字的笔痕。画面里没有按钮，也没有升级格子。
- **英文执行句**：Paint one ordinary illustration of a small fairy sitting on a wooden windowsill in a fairy garden, writing a letter, framed as a medium shot with the figure about sixty percent of the picture and a safe margin of window frame above the head, while the garden outside stays a soft far layer. Warm afternoon side light enters from the left casement and leaves a short warm contact shadow on the sill. Use opaque gouache with a matte surface and edges firmer than wet watercolor, while the forms stay connected. Match storybook-illustration finish with airy paint and no neon and no oily plastic highlights. Do not invent hair color, eye color, or wings, and keep the writing on the letter as faint unreadable marks. Include no buttons and no upgrade cells.
- **本套侧重**：中景窗台，午后暖侧光，哑光水粉。
- **相对原话改了什么**：把「窗边写信」收成可画的中景，并补上光和材质；身份细节仍标成还不确定。
- **建议交给**：`/pmem-draw`

## 方案 B · 信纸特写 · `route_id=draw.ordinary`

- **中文目标句**：画一张普通插画特写，镜头锁住小妖精的双手、一支羽毛笔和摊开的信纸，脸只带到下颌，窗边的妖精花园退成一块光。清晨偏冷的白日光从窗口斜落到纸面，纸上有一层薄而干净的高光，阴影偏青灰。媒介是水彩纸，纸纹和飞白留在信纸的空白处，手和笔的结构仍然连着。完成度对标绘本立绘，边缘松而形体不断，不要霓虹，不要油腻塑胶高光。因为脸没画全，所以不发明发色和瞳色；信上没有能读的字母。这是一张特写插画，不是道具格，也不是合成梯里的一档。
- **英文执行句**：Paint one ordinary illustration as a close-up locked on the small fairy's hands, a quill, and an open letter, with the face shown only to the chin and the fairy garden reduced to a patch of window light. Cool white morning daylight falls across the paper from the window, leaving a thin clean highlight and blue-gray shadows. Use watercolor paper so tooth and flying white remain in the blank margins while the hands and quill stay structurally connected. Match storybook-illustration finish with loose edges and unbroken forms, and use no neon and no oily plastic highlights. Do not invent hair color or eye color because the face is incomplete, and do not put readable letters on the page. This is a close-up illustration, not an item cell and not one step of a merge ladder.
- **本套侧重**：手与信纸的特写，清晨冷白光，水彩纸纹。
- **相对原话改了什么**：景别从整个人收到写信的手，光改成清晨冷白，材质改成带飞白的水彩纸。
- **建议交给**：`/pmem-draw`

## 方案 C · 窗框全景 · `route_id=draw.ordinary`

- **中文目标句**：画一张普通插画全景，近层是一整扇妖精花园的木窗，中层主体是坐在窗内写信的小妖精，远层是窗外的花、苔和园径。黄昏的逆光从窗外打来，妖精的轮廓带一圈暖金边，室内比窗外暗。媒介是色铅笔叠在齿纹纸上，纸齿看得见，轮廓用深褐色铅笔收住，边缘仍然松。完成度对标绘本立绘，结构连得住，不要霓虹，不要油腻塑胶高光。没被点名的发色、瞳色和翅膀都留白，不要另外画一个人；信纸上的字不可读。这是一张单张气氛插画，没有界面控件，也没有八格升级表。
- **英文执行句**：Paint one ordinary illustration in a wide view where the near layer is a whole wooden window of a fairy garden, the middle subject is a small fairy seated inside writing a letter, and the far layer is flowers, moss, and a garden path outside. Dusk backlight comes from beyond the glass, giving the fairy a warm gold rim while the room stays darker than the garden. Use colored pencil layered on toothy paper so the tooth stays visible and a deep brown pencil holds the contour, with edges that remain loose. Match storybook-illustration finish with connected structure, and use no neon and no oily plastic highlights. Leave unnamed hair color, eye color, and wings undecided, do not add a second person, and keep the letter unreadable. This is a single mood illustration with no interface controls and no eight-cell upgrade sheet.
- **本套侧重**：窗框全景的近中远三层，黄昏逆光金边，齿纹纸上的色铅笔。
- **相对原话改了什么**：把窗和花园拉开成三层景别，光改成黄昏逆光，材质改成色铅笔。
- **建议交给**：`/pmem-draw`
